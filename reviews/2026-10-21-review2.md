# TARS4 reviews #2, 2026-10-21 (after the review fixes and the demountable wiring)

Two read-only reviews by dedicated agents, same roles as `2026-10-21-review.md`. Nothing applied yet.
Consolidated open points (in French, for the user): `../questions_en_suspens.md`.

## Electronics

**Blocking**
- E2-B1 Dupont housings have no keying: X5 reversed puts VIN (VSYS) on LRC → R14 → GPIO17 (~40 mA); X1/X4 reversed put 3V3 on CS and GND on BLK; X2, X8, X15 too. Fix: key every 1×N housing (pull a header pin on the module + plug that cavity, or use a spare position); female side of inline pairs on the powered harness side; plug only with SW1 off, charger and S3 USB-C out. Document each X pin order.

**Important**
- E2-I1 R18 10 k only works with a ~1 M SD pull-up on the amp board (MAX98357A has 100 k internal pull-down); with a 100 k pull-up SD sits ~0.39 V on mains = (L+R)/2 mode, amp on. Measure; R18 ≤ R_pullup / 30 (2.2 k for 100 k); cut the trace if SD is tied to VIN.
- E2-I2 BL_EN PWM chops screen 2's backlight current on the shared 30 AWG 3V3 → ripple at the AM312. RC on AM312 VDD (100 Ω + 47 µF on P2) as standard, add C10 10 µF + C11 100 nF on P2 (P2 has no decoupling), PWM ~20 kHz, optional 1 nF on GPIO3.
- E2-I3 BLK pull-ups (10 k) draw ~0.66 mA for two screens in deep sleep with BL_EN held low: remove them if present.
- E2-I4 SCK still branched at P1 (E-I6): split at the carrier (two 47–68 Ω from GPIO12, one wire to screen 1, one straight into the middle tube; +1 local wire) or stay at 20–26.7 MHz.
- E2-I5 D1 leakage still open (E-I5): cathode on VPATH before SW1, reverse-biased by the cells all the time; warm leakage × ~9.4 k can lift VUSB 2–3 V (Q1 half off, USB_DET ambiguous). Measure at 4.2 V reverse, ~50 °C; > 50 µA → R1 4.7 k or lower-leakage Schottky.
- E2-I6 C6 must be X7R ceramic or film, not tantalum (0.5 µA leakage on a 500 k source = up to 0.5 V error).
- E2-I7 On mains while charging: 0.5 A charge + up to ~1 A system at J1 / X10; a weak source sags VUSB, Q1 runs on its body diode. Specify a 5 V ≥ 2 A charger; bench check VUSB ≥ 4.6 V at 1 A load while charging.
- E2-I8 Solder C7 on the amp's VIN/GND pins (module side of X5).

**Minor**: realistic sleep 0.35–0.65 mA (DS3231 80–110 µA, ADXL tap 50–140 µA, SD idle) — gate the microSD 3V3; clear ADXL INT_SOURCE and wait for PIR low before sleeping; TP4056 0.65–1 W + D1 on mains; SMAJ5.0A and electrolytic C9 correct (don't swap for ceramic); right cell path ~0.5 Ω vs 0.15 Ω (harmless); note "amp GND never on P2"; both S3 G pins on the carrier; hot-glue tack on housings after bench test.

**Docs**: README 438 "divider on P1" in the colour table; README 554 strapping list still names GPIO3; README 395 BLK sleep ~0.66 mA via the pull-ups (not R4); README 726 4 male housings (X3, X7, X14, X15), ~52 female contacts → buy 100; README 770–777 C9 (and C10/C11, RC) missing from capacitors to buy; README 871 pull-up total ≥ 1.1 k; schematic 157 RGB LED 0.5–1 vs 0.3–1 mA; kicad_sch_gen.py 285 D_TVS symbol bidirectional vs SMAJ5.0A unidirectional; X pin orders documented nowhere.

**Fine**: GPIO map, ADC + C6, I2C 100 kHz over 1 m, USB_DET divider, Q1/D1 orientation, TPS63020 load disconnect, PH/XH on power, R17 / R10.

**Open**: amp SD pull-up; BLK pull-up / current; D1 warm leakage; AM312 VOH ≥ 2.5 V; DW01 needs a first charger plug to switch on?; SW1 and charger rating; real sleep current ± microSD; SCK split decision; order status (D3, polyfuses, Dupont/JST, headers).

## Placement / routing

**Blocking**
- P2-B1 TP4056 cannot go into its slot: the upper groove lip covers x ≤ 3.1 / ≥ 27.3 at z 137.8–139.4, 0.3 mm above the PCB, where the unused USB-C (3.2 high), the IN/B/OUT pads (x ~3.7) and D3 + C9 (Ø5 × 11) sit. Fix: desolder the USB-C; lower ledge only (or lip ≤ 0.4 / notched over the pads); model C9 / D3 lying flat over the IN end; check the TPS63020 pads vs `bk_lip` too.

**Important**
- P2-I1 Top bundle has no path past X1 / X4 (z 216–227): straight-header housings 20.6 × 20.5 + screen tabs fill the section (23 wires come down in col1, 17 in col2). Fix: right-angle header on the screen back, pins down (housing flat, y 5.6–9.5, z ~205–222); add a bundle-corridor keep-out (tube end → P1/P2).
- P2-I2 Middle lid tabs (screen, P1/P2, S3) sweep x 1.6–4.7 where the bundles fall: pinching. Replace each pair of screen tabs with one continuous 2.6 mm rib; route bundles at x 5–25.
- P2-I3 Tie anchors still missing (P-I7): col0 ~112/180/215, col1 ~50/80/128, col2 ~100/178/212, col3 ~100/180, ≥ 25 mm from tube ends.
- P2-I4 Power block not serviceable: X10–X12 hidden in front of the TPS63020 / TP4056; three boards at once needs a jig. Park X10–X14 in the cavity z 141.5–169, y 4–28, x 3.4–25.4 (visible from the back); insert boards one by one with 4–5 cm loops.
- P2-I5 Inline boxes lack end bend room: add 2 × bend to inline lengths; X15 ≥ z 105 (clear of the wire-slot exit), move X13.
- P2-I6 C1 has no modelled place (Ø6.3 can > 6 mm carrier gap): ≤ 5 mm polymer / tantalum 7343 flat on the carrier, or a can beside the lug z 47–55; model C7 behind the amp.
- P2-I7 Power-leg vents still missing (P-I2): lid slots z ~136–145 and 215–230; thermocouple; PETG for col0?

**Minor**: S3 service loop 6–8 cm; left middle lid variant without speaker tabs; carrier wires inward of x 5 / 25, carrier top band (antenna) free; BOOT button only with the S3 out (document); missing from kos: cradles, spare slot, screen frame, S3 ribs/strips, switch frame, microSD card path; header tails ≤ 1 mm (X5, X2, X9; BME280 has no gap); tape X3 / X7; crimping needs ≥ 5 cm pulled out; continuity check before closing.

**Fine**: AM312 clearances, tube-end twisting, fuses, DS3231 notch, X2/X3/X5–X9, S3 stack, wire channel area, leg lids.

**Docs**: README 339 TPS63020 "z ≈ 133" → 124.7; "power block slides in one go" → board by board; "its own USB-C unused" → removed; 4 male housings; JST-XH pitch 2.50; "tie at the anchors" none modelled; "checked against the pivot zone" overstated (bundle paths unchecked).

**Open**: TP4056 / TPS63020 pad positions vs lips; back of the screens (FPC, parts) before a right-angle header; measured Dupont / PH / XH lengths; C1 part; PETG col0; second middle lid; leg swing angle.
