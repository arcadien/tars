## Important

- E-I1 Deep sleep ~1–2 mA, not < 0.2 mA: S3 Mini RGB LED (0.3–1 mA even dark), DS3231 32K output on (clear EN32kHz, reg 0x0F bit 3), DS3231 VCC standby ~100 µA, SD standby, D1 leakage. Remove RGB LED, clear EN32kHz, gate SD power → ~0.2–0.4 mA.
- E-I2 SPI pins float in deep sleep: gpio_hold on CS1/CS2/RST (or 100 k pull-ups on CS1/CS2 on P1).
- E-I3 PIR inverter no longer needed (SQW gone), BS170 marginal: AM312 OUT straight to GPIO3 (1 k series at P2), INT1 default active high, GPIO2/3/21 on EXT1 ANY_HIGH (drop GPIO21 from the mask on mains).
- E-I4 D3 placement contradictory; hot-plug ringing at the TP4056 input 16 cm up the leg: D3 at the TP4056 IN pads (+ 10–47 µF electrolytic), optional second TVS at J1.
- E-I5 D1 leakage (warm) through R1 / R15-R16, permanent even with SW1 off; USB_DET may float 0.6–1.8 V: measure hot, R1 4.7 k or lower-leakage Schottky, R15 56 k.
- E-I6 SPI with a T at P1: SCK point-to-point (FSPICLK routed to a second pad, GPIO4 if free, 47–68 Ω each) or stay at 20–26.7 MHz.
- E-I7 I2C crosstalk in the tubes: 100 kHz + glitch filter, twist SDA/GND and SCL/3V3 in the right tube, SCL away from SCK.
- P-I1 F2 has no place in col3 (DS3231 bottom stop at 105.1–106.7 takes the front wall; 4.6 mm left above the top shelf): F2 flat on the shelf behind the wire slot or a notch in the RTC stop; F1/F2 dummies + check; pre-form B+/B− before inserting the RTC.
- P-I2 Heat in the power leg: R_PROG 2.4 k, lid vents (z 136–141 and 215–230), optionally TP4056 in band 190–207.
- P-I3 JST-PH (2.0) does not fit stripboard: board-mounted XH / KF2510 (side-entry behind P1/P2), PH inline only; no headers above flat boards (4 mm) or behind the S3 (6 mm).
- P-I4 The S3 is not removable (22–26 wires in a 6 mm gap): 6–10 cm pigtails grouped in 3–4 inline connectors parked in col1 z 43–99, or d1_standoff 6 → 12.
- P-I5 Antenna keep-out at the S3's top end (z 35–42.5): route along the long edges.
- P-I6 AM312 false triggers on Wi-Fi bursts: RC on its VDD at P2 (100 Ω + 10–47 µF), 1 nF on GPIO3.
- P-I7 Wires pinched by the lid / no strain relief: printed cable-tie anchors (col0 ~112/145/180/215, col1 ~50/98/178, col2 ~98/178, col3 ~100/180), 25–30 mm free on the turning side of each tube.
- P-I8 The 1 A path goes down to the floor switch and back (~0.35 m × 2, ~90 mV): switch on the TPS63020 EN instead (to check: VSYS stays live, back-feed).

## Minor

- Back-feed of 3V3 through USB_DET with SW1 off on mains (rail at ~1.5–2 V, harmless).
- ADC: 0.1–1 % resistors, one-point calibration, 12 dB attenuation + adc_cali.
- microSD supply loop: 3V3 local but GND from P1 → take both from the same place.
- Brass tubes: deburr / chamfer, thin PTFE or heat-shrink liner; optional 1 M bleed to GND.
- Inrush at SW1 on: fine, don't add bulk capacitance. Charging from a PC port exceeds 500 mA.
- KiCad: D3 symbol bidirectional (SMAJ5.0A is unidirectional); J1 only a note.
- microSD lid slot: lead-in funnel. Screen header orientation; MAX98357A screw terminal off; speaker terminals vs lid tabs; DS3231 service loop (CR2032 faces the wall); sleeve wires in the cradle grooves (steel can); P1/P2 face orientation (copper front, connectors to the lid); middle joint wear.
- Cable lengths plausible (+1.5 m 30 AWG with pigtails).

## Suggested assembly order (placement review)

1. Bench, outside TARS: power module (J1 + D3, TP4056, power-path board, switch, F1/F2 + plates on flying leads), TPS63020 with pigtail, P1/P2, S3 with pigtails, each peripheral with pigtail + connector.
2. Print, glue halves + splints, check grooves.
3. Front-wall parts first (screens, ADXL345, INMP441, amp, BME280, DS3231 with B+ pre-formed, speaker, charge port, switch, F1/F2 + plates).
4. Tubes glued in their fixed column, bare harness threaded, collars.
5. Flat boards bottom-up from the back (microSD, TPS63020, TP4056, power-path), then P1/P2, then the S3 last.
6. Crimp / plug the connectors, tie at anchors, service loops, 25–30 mm free on turning sides.
7. Close the lids (nothing over a post), test 3.9, cells last.
