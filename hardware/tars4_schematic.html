TARS4 — schematic

LOLIN S3 Mini version, with microSD, motion sensor and weather sensor. Functional schematic: blocks are arranged by role, not by position in the columns (each block's column is recalled in grey). Proposed GPIO map, to confirm when soldering.

  • VUSB (5 V from the charge port)
  • cells B+
  • cells B−
  • VSYS (after the switch)
  • GND (TP4056 OUT−)
  • 3V3
  • screens SPI
  • I2S amplifier + mic
  • I2C + interrupts
  • BL_EN (backlight) · AMP_SD (amplifier)
  • microSD (SDMMC 1-bit)
  • speaker
  • branch made on a P1 / P2 board

Distribution boards P1 and P2

Stripboards at 2.54 mm pitch, horizontal strips, standing under each screen (slot modelled in tars4.scad, MEASURE dimensions: 24 × 32 × 1.6), copper side towards the lid. P1 no longer carries any battery voltage (the sense divider is in the leg): a solder bridge can neither put VSYS on the 3V3 nor short the battery. The TPS63020 3.3 V arrives on P1 and leaves for the S3 and every module (the microSD gets it from Q5, on the power-path board); all the logic GND goes through P1; the amplifier has its own VSYS/GND pair from the left leg, and the microSD its dedicated GND (from P1). Single-destination signals go straight from the S3 carrier: CS1, CS2, SCK of each screen (split on the carrier by R12 / R19), I2S of the amplifier and the mic, INT1 and the 3 microSD signals.

GPIO map (proposed)

GPIOSignalToRemark

Free: 37 (36 = SD_EN; 37 planned for the VSYS sense, PLAT-POWER-VSENSE-001). Avoid: 0, 45, 46 (strapping; GPIO3 is the motion sensor input, harmless as long as the JTAG eFuse is not burnt), 19, 20 (USB), 43, 44 (serial console), 47 (board RGB LED). 33 to 37 are free on the S3FH4R2 (quad PSRAM).

Notes

  • B− is not GND: the DW01 and its MOSFETs (FS8205A) sit between them. Everything else refers to OUT− (GND).
  • Star ground on the power-path board (left leg): one logic GND to P1, the GND of the amplifier pair, and the dedicated microSD GND leaves from P1. Twisted pairs: B+/B−, amplifier VSYS/GND, microSD CLK/GND.
  • P1: check with a multimeter that no neighbouring strips are bridged before sliding the board in.
  • Battery cut-off at 3.3 V read on B+ (GPIO1, under load): the TPS63020 holds 3.3 V down to ~2.5 V input, the cut-off only spares the cells. No more reset risk on the Wi-Fi peak.
  • On mains, VUSB turns Q1 off (gate at 5 V) and feeds VSYS through D1 (≈ 4.7 V); the cells charge on their own. On battery, R1 pulls the gate to 0 V and Q1 conducts.
  • The S3 V pin (VBUS) only gets its own USB-C (its contact is removed from the carrier header): the PC 5 V only feeds the S3 internal regulator, never VSYS nor the cells. Flash with the switch on or off. On USB, the internal regulator and the TPS63020 feed the 3V3 together (the TPS63020 in power-save mode does not fight it). On battery, a little 3.3 V leaks back through the internal regulator to VBUS, as on any ESP32 board powered from its 3V3 pin (to confirm in the ME6211 datasheet). Firmware: ignore the cut-off if B+ < 2.5 V, and keep BL_EN and AMP_SD low until the supply is validated.
  • BL_EN (GPIO7) only drives the backlights now: PWM possible (LEDC) to set the brightness. R4 holds it low at boot, gpio_hold in sleep. Don't solder it to the S3 EN pad (that's its reset, next to GPIO1). AMP_SD (GPIO4) has its own line: R10 1 k in series and R18 10 k to GND on P2, the amplifier stays off at boot and in sleep whatever is on BLK; when high, SD > 1.4 V = left channel (same sample on both channels). To check: SD pull-up on the amplifier board (Adafruit: 1 M).
  • Amplifier on I2S0 (BCLK, LRC, DIN), mic on I2S1 with its own clocks (GPIO 14, 38, 15), wired locally: neither imposes a sample rate on the other.
  • Screens: SPI started at 26.7 MHz (one frame in 35 ms per screen), 40 MHz to try afterwards (ST7789 ~62 MHz max); SCK split on the S3 carrier: R12 47 Ω → screen 1, R19 47 Ω → screen 2 through its own wire (no more branch at P1), up to 100 Ω if the image jitters; screen 2 SCK wire next to the screen GND in the tube, away from BCLK; GPIO12 with reduced drive strength. Shared RST: RST = -1 in the library and one manual reset.
  • microSD: on a reader labelled for SPI, CLK = SCK, CMD = DI/MOSI, D0 = DO/MISO, D3 = CS (pull-up). 10 MHz, R11 33 Ω in series on CLK, C4/C5 at the reader.
  • DS3231 ZS-042 with a CR2032: remove the cell charging resistor. In the right leg (z ≈ 107), SQW not wired: the S3 wakes on its own timer, the RTC keeps the time. ADXL345: CS to 3V3 (I2C), SDO to GND (0x53). Sleep: the ZS-042 LED and the RGB LED (GPIO47) draw current; send SLPIN to the screens.
  • Ground: the TPS63020 has its own VSYS/GND pair from the star; the logic GND to P1 leaves from its GND pad and runs along its 3V3 wire (a single loop). The amplifier has its pair, the microSD its GND from P1.
  • Switch off, S3 on the PC USB: all the 3V3 goes through the S3 regulator (500 mA). Firmware: don't start the amplifier I2S nor raise AMP_SD / BL_EN until the supply is validated (VSYS sense on GPIO37, PLAT-POWER-VSENSE-001). Check that the TPS63020 does not start from its output (VSYS must stay < 2.5 V) and the max input voltage of the unpowered MAX98357A.
  • Never drive the TPS63020 PS/SYNC high: in forced PWM it can sink current and send the S3 regulator's current back to the cells.
  • To measure: ME6211 reverse current into VBUS on battery (cable plugged into a PC that is off), and total sleep current (power_budget.md).
  • Sleep: aim at ~0.2–0.4 mA. Desolder the S3 RGB LED (GPIO47, 0.3–1 mA even off), disable the DS3231 32 kHz output (EN32kHz, register 0x0F bit 3), gpio_hold on CS1 / CS2 / RST / BL_EN / AMP_SD. Deep discharge: after the 3.3 V cut-off ~5–10 % of capacity is left; the TPS (25–50 µA), the R2/R3 divider (2 µA), the SD card (0.1–0.5 mA), the GPIO47 RGB LED (0.3–1 mA) and above all the ZS-042 LED (1–3 mA) drain it in 1–3 weeks. Remove every status LED fed by the battery (ZS-042, TPS63020 module, GY-291, microSD reader…) keeping the TP4056 charge LEDs (fed by USB only), and don't light the RGB LED; store an "empty" flag in RTC memory (no wake-up until recharged); Q5 cuts the microSD 3V3 (SD_EN high or floating); clear the ADXL345 INT_SOURCE and wait for PIR low before sleeping.
  • Motion sensor: AM312 at the top of the right middle, active-high push-pull output straight onto the PIR wire through R17 1 k (P2), 3V3 / GND taken from P2, the wire goes back through the middle tube to GPIO3. No inverter any more: wake-up is EXT1 ANY_HIGH with INT1 and USB_DET. R21 + C12 filter its supply; backlight PWM at ~20 kHz. Uses: wake on approach, lock the PC (BLE keyboard) when leaving.
  • Connectors: the wires of this schematic go through X1–X16 (no X11 any more) to the modules and the S3 plugs into its carrier: the schematic is unchanged. Both ends of every wire and the assembly order are in the wiring guide (tars4_harness.html).
  • Weather sensor: BME280 on the I2C bus (0x76; DS3231 0x68 + EEPROM 0x57, ADXL345 0x53), in the right leg with the DS3231, away from the S3, the TPS63020 and the charger; SDA / SCL / 3V3 / GND from P2 through the right tube (SDA / SCL cross the middle tube from the ADXL345, each twisted with a supply wire in the right tube). The boards' pull-ups add up (~1.6–2.4 k with those of the DS3231, BME280 and ADXL345); I2C at 100 kHz with the glitch filter: fine at 100–400 kHz, remove one pair if needed. Remove its LED if it has one.
  • Charger detection: USB_DET (GPIO21) is high when a cable is plugged into the charge port. The firmware thus knows it is on mains (display, no battery cut-off) and can wake on plug-in (ext0 or ext1). End of charge is deduced from B+ (GPIO1), which falls back from 4.2 to ~4.15 V.
  • Wake-up: on the ESP32-S3, EXT1 accepts one polarity only. Everything is active high: ADXL345 INT1 (default), PIR (AM312) and USB_DET → EXT1 ANY_HIGH on GPIO2, 3 and 21; remove from the mask any source already high when going to sleep (GPIO21 on mains, GPIO3 while the PIR holds), otherwise the S3 wakes at once. Timed wake-up uses the S3 timer.
