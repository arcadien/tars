TARS4 — wiring, back view

TARS lying on its belly, lids removed. Seen from the back, the left leg is on the right of the drawing. Dimensions in mm, to scale; wires are schematic (they run behind the boards, on the lid side).

Wires: actual kit colours

Power in pairs: red / black for the battery and 5 V (B+/B−, VUSB, VPATH, VSYS and their GND; each rail carries a coloured band at both ends, on the + and on its GND: none = B+ / B−, white = OUT+, yellow = VPATH, orange = VSYS, purple = VUSB), white / blue for the regulated 3V3 and its logic GND; blue is only used for that GND, never for a signal. 30 AWG, 10-colour kit: one colour per signal role, the same on every segment; heat-shrink rings at both ends (strokes across on the drawing) tell the buses apart: none = screens / measurements, 1 = I2S (amplifier and mic) and PIR, 2 = I2C, 3 = microSD. The coloured cable ties of the kit can replace the rings at the ends, never closer than 25–30 mm to a tube end. 26 AWG for power (thick strokes).

Nets

  • Pack B+ / B−2 × 18650 in parallel, twisted pair, right cell → charger through the 3 tubes
  • +VSYS / GNDcells (TP4056 OUT+ via AO3401A) or mains (micro-USB socket in the foot via D1 1N5822) → floor switch → star on the power-path board (z ≈ 171) → TPS63020 (3.3 V); dedicated twisted VSYS/GND pair → amplifier VIN. GND = TP4056 OUT− (≠ B−, the DW01 sits between them)
  • 3V3 / logic GNDTPS63020 output (left leg) → P1 → S3 3V3 pin (26 AWG white / blue); screens, RTC, accelerometer, mic, sensors in 30 AWG white / blue; microSD locally
  • Screen SPI ×5SCK, MOSI, DC, CS, RST per screen; SCK split on the S3 carrier (R12 → screen 1, R19 → screen 2, 47 Ω), no T on P1
  • I2S ×3 (1 ring)I2S0: BCLK, LRC, DIN → amplifier (middle tube); I2S1: SCK, WS, SD ← mic, local (mic L/R to GND)
  • I2C ×2 (2 rings)daisy chain: S3 → ADXL345 (0x53) → middle tube → P2 → right tube → BME280 (0x76) and DS3231 (0x68, EEPROM 0x57) in the right leg; pull-ups already on the boards; SQW not wired
  • microSD ×3 (3 rings)SDMMC 1-bit: CLK (33 Ω at the S3), CMD, D0 → reader in the left leg, + 3V3 cut in sleep by Q5 (SD_EN, GPIO36) and a dedicated GND from P1 (twisted with CLK); 10 k pull-ups (to the switched 3V3) on CMD, D0, D1, D2, D3 if the reader has none; 10 µF + 100 nF at the reader; 10 MHz
  • BL_EN · AMP_SDGPIO7 → BLK of both screens (backlight, PWM possible); GPIO4 → R10 1 k + R18 10 k on P2 → amplifier SD (off at boot and in sleep). Not the S3 EN pad (reset)
  • Motion sensorAM312 behind a Ø10 hole in the front (top of the right middle, z ≈ 233), 1.25 mm right of the column axis; R17 1 k on P2 (under screen 2), 3 wires to the sensor; PIR (active high, red 1 ring) goes back through the middle tube to GPIO3
  • VBAT/2 (brown) · USB_DET (orange) · VSENSE_B (brown, 1 ring)dividers on the power-path board → GPIO1 / GPIO21, through the left tube; VSYS through R23 1 M (in line at the board) → Q4 on the carrier → GPIO37
  • Weather sensorBME280 (I2C 0x76) in the right leg, the coolest spot (z ≈ 190), sensor facing a Ø1.5 vent in the front; SDA / SCL / 3V3 / GND from P2 through the right tube, with the DS3231 (z ≈ 107)

The harness is soldered once (P1, P2, S3 carrier, power-path board, cell plates, tube wires); every module plugs into it. TP4056 + power path + TPS63020 make a power block soldered and tested on the bench, linked by X10–X14, parked in the free cavity above the TP4056 (visible from the back, lid open); the boards slide in one by one with a 4–5 cm loop. Female side of the inline pairs = powered harness side. Plug / unplug only with the switch off, charger and S3 USB-C unplugged. 3 to 5 cm loop at each module to pull it out from the back before unplugging it. Volumes checked in the model and drawn in orange in the sections (renders/tars4_column_sections.svg).

Notes

Power: TPS63020 (buck-boost, 3.3 V, EN to VIN, PS/SYNC to GND) in the left leg, 3.3 V straight to the S3 3V3 pin. Software cut-off at 3.3 V on B+ (VBAT/2 on GPIO1). The S3 internal regulator is only used on USB. Firmware: ignore the cut-off if B+ < 2.5 V or if USB_DET is high (S3 powered from the PC USB, switch off), and keep BL_EN and AMP_SD low until the supply is validated.

Resettable fuses F1, F2 (PPTC 30 V, 1.6 A) on the + of each cell, flat on the upper shelf of each leg (z 100.5–103.7); D3 (TVS) and C9 (22 µF) on the TP4056 IN pads, R_PROG 2.4 k (0.5 A charge); twisted pairs B+/B−, amplifier VSYS/GND and microSD CLK/GND; tubes deburred, heat-shrink at the tube exits.

BL_EN (GPIO7) only drives the backlight (BLK) of both screens: PWM possible. R4 10 k holds it low at boot; PWM at ~20 kHz. The AM312 has its own filter on P2 (R21 100 Ω + C12 10 µF tantalum), and P2 its decoupling (C10 10 µF + C11 100 nF). AMP_SD (GPIO4) has its own wire: R10 1 k in series and R18 10 k to GND on P2, the amplifier stays off at boot and in sleep; high → left channel: send the same sample on both channels.

Mains: a USB charger on the micro-USB socket of the left foot powers TARS (≈ 4.7 V on VSYS) while the cells charge on their own.

Before joining the two cells: same model, same age, voltages equal within 0.05 V.

The S3 VBUS pin only gets its own USB-C: the PC 5 V never goes back to the cells, flash with the switch on or off.

ADXL345 and mic in the controller column, wired locally; DS3231 and BME280 in the right leg, through the right tube (I2C ×2, 3V3, GND from P2). Every module plugs in through a connector (see the table above).

DS3231 ZS-042 with a CR2032 (not rechargeable): remove the charging resistor (200 Ω) or the diode near the coin-cell holder, otherwise the board tries to charge the cell.

ADXL345 on I2C: CS → 3V3, SDO → GND (address 0x53); INT1 → GPIO2 for wake on tap. Pins cut flush on the wall side.

INMP441 mic: chip side against the wall, the hole of the metal can facing the Ø1 hole drilled in the vertical groove. Adhesive foam gasket around it, never glue on the hole.

microSD: 3.3 V reader without regulator, flat in the left leg (z ≈ 117), under the TPS63020 and the TP4056 (z ≈ 125 / 136), socket towards the lid; the card goes in through the slot of the left leg lid (part lid_leg_sd), without opening. ~30 cm of wire from the S3: SDMMC at 10 MHz, R11 33 Ω on CLK. Free GPIOs of your choice (the ESP32-S3 matrix routes SDMMC anywhere). Its 3V3 goes through Q5 (AO3401A, R22 100 k on the gate) on the power-path board: cut in sleep, GPIO36 (SD_EN) low = on; set CLK / CMD / D0 as inputs before cutting.

Switch recessed in the floor, lever 0.5 mm back: flip it with a pen tip through the slot.

Screen 2 RST goes through the tube (forgotten in the old count of 6 SPI wires).
